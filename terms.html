Terms of use

Draft · last updated October 2026

Byome is a small community for honest beauty reviews. These terms are written in plain language. By making an account, you agree to them.

Your account

  • Byome is for adults: you need to be at least 18 years old to make an account, and you confirm this when you sign up. If we learn that a member is under 18, we'll remove their account.
  • Keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what's posted from your account.
  • One account per person, please. Points, achievements and shop items can't be sold or traded for money.

What you post

  • Reviews should be about products you've actually used, in your own words. Positive and negative reviews are equally welcome.
  • If a brand gave you a product for free or paid you, say so in your review.
  • You keep ownership of what you write. By posting, you let Byome show it on the site, including after you delete your account if you choose to keep your reviews up (they then show as "Deleted member").
  • Don't post anything that's hateful, harassing, sexual, illegal, spam, someone else's private information, or someone else's work without permission.
  • Beauty reviews share personal experiences, not medical advice. Talk to a professional about skin conditions, allergies or reactions.

Moderation

Moderators can hold, edit out or remove posts, reviews, comments, profile pictures and usernames that break these terms, and can remove points earned through them. Accounts that keep breaking the rules can be suspended or removed. You can report anything you think breaks the rules with the flag button.

Points and the biome

Community points and Byome points have no cash value. Byome points buy digital pots, plants and shelves for your profile. Community points can't be spent on anything yet; we'll share more when we have something to announce. We may adjust point values, prices and items as the site grows, and points earned by breaking the rules can be taken back.

Inviting friends

You can invite friends with your own invite link or code. When a friend you invited signs up, writes a review and makes a post or comment, you both earn a bonus; you can earn bonuses for up to 5 friends a week. Please only invite people you know: no spamming, and no buying, selling or trading codes. Each email address can only be invited once. Bonuses earned by misusing invites, for example with fake accounts, can be taken back, and we may change the bonus amounts or end the program.

Ads on Byome

Byome is free, and a small number of ads help keep it running. Ads only ever appear in the Byome Shop. They will never appear on Reviews or in the Community, and every ad is clearly labeled "Advertisement." Ads are chosen by our ad provider, not by us, so an ad doesn't mean we recommend that product or brand. Advertisers have no say in what members write, and ads never affect which reviews are shown or how they're ranked. See our privacy policy for how ads use cookies.

Changes and the beta

Byome is in beta, so features may change, break or disappear. We'll update these terms as the site grows, and change the date at the top when we do.
