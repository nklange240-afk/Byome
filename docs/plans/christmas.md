# Plan: the Christmas event (agreed so far, not built yet)

Byome's biggest event yet, running about five weeks. The decisions are the owner's. Anything marked **(to decide)** is still open.

## Dates (Central time)
- **Starts Friday November 27** (Black Friday, the day after Thanksgiving), just after midnight.
- **Black Friday sale:** Friday November 27 to Monday November 30 (Cyber Monday), 11:59pm.
- **Ends Thursday December 31**, 11:59pm. There's no separate New Year event.
- **Grace period:** about two weeks into January, like Halloween's, so things started during the event can be finished.
- **New Year (maybe):** a small gift, for example a **disco ball pot**.

## Welcome gift: the Christmas tree
- **A mini Christmas tree for the medium pots,** given to every member the first time they visit the Events page during the event.
- **Heirloom,** because it's only given during Christmas 2026 and never sold afterwards. It's something kept and brought out every year. Its description says so, and its info says "Christmas 2026".
- **Decoration themes, chosen with coloured circles** under the tree (in the inventory and in Arrange), not separate items:
  - The tree comes with **Classic** and **Snowflake**, plus **Bare** (a plain little pine, so it can stay up all year).
  - **More themes are unlocked during the event,** some from the advent calendar and some bought with Byome points.
  - Locked themes show as faded circles with a lock and how to get them.
  - Each circle is split into two colours and has its name for screen readers, so it doesn't rely on colour alone.
  - When someone taps the tree on a member's shelf, it shows the chosen theme, like "Christmas tree, Snowflake".
- **Theme ideas:** Classic (red/gold), Snowflake (blue/white), **Candlelight** (vintage clip-on candles, white and gold, owner's idea), **Candy Cane**, **Pastel**. More may come from Woodland, Rustic, Gingerbread, Silver & Frost and Retro. **(to decide: the final list)**
- **Themes can be added later,** including next Christmas, for trees members already own.

## Shelf decorations with the same themes
- **The paper garland and the hanging stockings** could use the same theme circles: unlocking a theme unlocks it for every themed item, and members choose for each item separately, so they can match or mix. **(to decide: which items get themes)**
- They're kept thin, along the shelf edges, so a decorated tree plus a garland doesn't feel cluttered.

## Black Friday sale
- **New winter and Christmas items arrive in the shop** on Friday November 27, together on a "Black Friday" shelf at the top of the shop for the weekend.
- **30% off until Cyber Monday,** then full price. The items stay in the shop afterwards.
- **One doorbuster at 60% off, Friday only.** **(to decide: which item)**
- **In the shop:** the old price is crossed out next to the sale price, with a "Sale ends Monday" tag written in words, not only in colour.
- **Enforced by the database:** members pay the sale price right up to the deadline and never after. It ends on its own, with no SQL to run.
- **To build:** sale prices and an end date for each item. The shop already supports items appearing on a set date.

## The advent calendar
- **24 stamps over the event's five weeks.** Days don't need to be in a row, so members can miss up to 11 days and still finish. This encourages frequent visits without needing every day.
- **Stamped on the Events page:** a calendar with 24 doors, and today's door to tap.
- **A reminder banner** the first time a member visits each day during the event, until they've stamped today's door: "Your advent calendar has a door waiting 🎄", linking to the Events page.
- **This replaces the 7-stamp calendar** that was built into the daily surprise for Christmas. The daily surprise carries on as normal, separately.
- **Rewards (a starting suggestion, to decide):**
  - a few Byome points on most doors
  - a tree theme on some doors (for example 6, 12 and 18)
  - a special item on door 24 (for example the garland, or a calendar-only theme)
- **Christmas Day (optional idea):** a small gift for everyone who visits on December 25.

## Challenges and achievements
- **Ghosts of Christmas Past:** review a product that still haunts you, good or bad. For example:
  - the skincare that broke you out so badly you still have nightmares
  - the fragrance you loved in the shop but couldn't justify buying
  - the discontinued product you've never found a dupe for
- **On the review form,** the event version of "I no longer use this" becomes **"This is a Ghost of Christmas Past 👻"**, with a short hint covering all three kinds. Reviews with it are tagged and listed on the Events page, like Halloween's.
- **To build:** event checkboxes per event. They're currently fixed to Halloween's ("I no longer use this", "This is a fall or seasonal product"). Christmas could have its own second checkbox, for example "This is a winter or holiday product".
- **Points for helping are only for receiving:** members earn points when others find their reviews helpful, never for handing out Helpful marks. Rewards are hidden ("A surprise, coming soon") until their art is ready. Things finished during the event still count during the grace period.

## New feature: "Want to try" list
Introduced by Making a List, Checking It Twice, and kept after the event.
- **A "Want to try" button** on every product page and review, to save the product to your list.
- **Your list:** each product with its rating, how many reviews it has, and **how many new reviews since you added it**, so it's easy to check in.
- **Notifications (optional):** "3 new reviews of a product on your list", sent at most once a day so it never gets noisy.
- **Tried it?** Reviewing a product on your list offers to move it to a "Tried" section.
- **(to decide)** Whether lists are private (the suggestion, at least to start) or can be shown on your profile, and whether there's a limit on length.
- **Weekly drops (suggestion):** a new theme or shop item each week, announced on the Events page, so the event stays fresh for five weeks.
