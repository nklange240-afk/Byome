About Byome

Byome is a free community for honest beauty reviews, written by the people actually using the products.

Why Byome exists

Finding beauty products that really work is hard. Reviews elsewhere are crowded with sponsored posts, and it's tough to tell a genuine opinion from an ad. Byome is a calmer, kinder place to share what's working for you, what isn't, and who a product might suit.

Byome is founded and run by people who have spent years building their own routines. We understand the highs, lows and struggles of finding products that work, and we wanted somewhere to talk about them honestly.

Honest, by design

  • Every opinion counts. Reviews are rewarded for being detailed and thoughtful, whether they're positive or negative.
  • Gifted products are labeled. If a reviewer got a product for free or was paid by the brand, their review says so.
  • Brands don't get a say. Advertisers have no influence over what members write, and ads never change which reviews you see. Ads only appear in the Byome Shop.
  • Real people, kept safe. Byome is for adults (18+), moderated by real people, with an automod to catch the worst, and no links from members, to keep spam out.

What you can do on Byome

  • Read and write reviews of makeup, skincare, hair care, fragrance, body care and nails, rated from 1 to 5 leaves.
  • Join the Community to ask questions, share your SOTD, start polls and celebrate wins.
  • Grow your shelf. Taking part earns Byome points to spend on pots, plants and biomes for your profile, and seasonal events bring limited-edition treats.

Hand-drawn, not generated

Every plant, pot and shelf on Byome is drawn by hand by Byome's founder. No AI art, just a lot of love (and a lot of leaves).

Growing together

Byome is small, independent and still growing, and it's shaped by its members. Spotted a bug or have an idea? Send us feedback, or email hello@byome.xyz. You can find more answers in our FAQ.
