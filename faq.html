Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know about Byome. Can't find your answer? Message the moderators through Mod mail, or read about Byome.

Joining Byome

What is Byome?

Byome is a free community for honest beauty reviews. Members review the makeup, skincare, hair care, fragrance, body care and nail products they actually use, chat in the Community, and grow a little shelf of hand-drawn plants as they take part.

Is Byome free?

Yes. Byome is free to join and use. A few ads help keep it running, and they only ever appear in the Byome Shop, never on reviews or in the Community.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 18 or older. You just need an email address and a username.

Do I need an account to read reviews?

No. Anyone can read reviews and see what's on in Events. You'll need a free account to write reviews, join the Community and grow your shelf.

Reviews

How do ratings work?

Products are rated from 1 to 5 leaves. You can also say whether you'd buy it again, and mark a 5-leaf product as a Holy grail if it's one you'd never be without.

Can I post a negative review?

Absolutely. Honest reviews are what Byome is for, and positive and negative reviews are equally welcome. Reviews are rewarded for being detailed and thoughtful, not for being glowing.

I got the product for free. Can I still review it?

Yes, please do! Just tick “Gifted” when you write your review if a brand gave you the product, paid you, or you're connected to the brand in any other way. Your review will show a Gifted tag, so everyone knows.

The product I want to review isn't on Byome. What do I do?

Suggest it! You can add a new product while writing your review, or from Suggest a product in the menu. A moderator checks new products, usually within a day, and your review goes live as soon as the product is approved.

Can I update a review later?

Yes. You can edit your review, or add an update to it, for example after using a product for a few more months. Updates show underneath the original review.

Byome points, shelves and levels

What are Byome points?

Byome points are earned for taking part: writing reviews, getting product suggestions approved, inviting friends, spotting bugs and typos, and more. You spend them in the Byome Shop on pots, plants, shelves and biomes for your profile.

What is the plant shelf?

Every profile has a shelf of pots and plants. You get your first plant for joining, earn more from achievements and events, and buy others in the Byome Shop. You can arrange your shelf however you like.

What are biomes?

Biomes are backgrounds for your profile, like a forest, a meadow or a snowy tundra. Each has its own scenery and little animated touches.

What are levels?

As you take part, you grow from Seed to Sprout, Sapling, Bloom and Evergreen. Levels show how much you've helped Byome grow, mostly through reviews.

What is the daily surprise?

Once a day you can pick a seed packet and shake out a small prize: Byome points or, now and then, an item for your shelf.

What are events?

Seasonal events add achievements, extra Byome points and limited-edition pots and plants for a few weeks at a time. Check the Events page to see what's on.

Posting in the Community

Why is my post waiting for a moderator?

Byome's automod holds back posts, comments and reviews that contain certain words, so a moderator can take a quick look before everyone sees them. If it's fine, it's approved, usually quickly.

Why can't I post links?

To keep out spam and scams, only moderators can post links to other websites. You can still name a brand or shop, and links to byome.xyz are always fine.

How do I report something?

Use the flag button on any post, comment, review or profile. Reports go straight to the moderators, who take a look.

Can I block someone?

Yes. Use Block on their profile. You won't see each other's posts, comments or reviews, and they aren't told.

Your account

Can I change my username?

Yes, on Edit profile. Your old username is kept for you for 7 days, so nobody else can take it and you can switch back if you change your mind.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Edit profile and scroll to Delete account. Your personal details are deleted for good. Your reviews, posts and comments stay up as written by “Deleted member”, unless you tick the box to delete them too.

How do I contact Byome?

Message the moderators through Mod mail, use Send feedback at the bottom of any page, or email hello@byome.xyz.
