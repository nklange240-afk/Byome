Privacy policy

Draft · last updated October 2026

We collect as little as we need to run Byome, and we don't sell your information.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, username, password (stored scrambled, so nobody can read it), and when you confirmed you're 18 or older.
  • Your profile, if you add them: a profile picture, ring color, and skin and hair details. These are optional and shown on your profile.
  • What you do on the site: your reviews, posts, comments, likes, reports, points, achievements and biome items.
  • Invites: your invite code, who invited you (if anyone) and who you've invited. So the same email address can't be invited twice, we also keep a scrambled one-way fingerprint of each email address used to sign up. It can't be turned back into your email, and it stays even if you delete your account.
  • On your device: your browser remembers that you're logged in, and which achievement pop-ups you've already seen.

Byome is for adults

You need to be 18 or older to make an account, and we keep a record that you confirmed this when you signed up. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we'll delete the account and its information.

Who can see it

Your username, profile picture, profile details, reviews, posts, comments, shelf and achievements are public to other members. Your email address, points history, invites and reports are private (the friends you invite can see that you invited them when they sign up). Moderators can see reports and content waiting for review.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores the site's data and handles logins.
  • Resend sends our emails (account confirmation and password resets).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google AdSense shows the ads in the Byome Shop (see below).
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr provide the fonts and a code library, so your browser connects to them when pages load.

Ads in the Byome Shop

The Byome Shop shows ads from Google AdSense, inside the sign labeled "Advertisement." Ads appear only in the shop, never on Reviews, in the Community or anywhere else on Byome.

On the shop page only, Google may use cookies and similar technology to show ads, measure them, and limit how often you see the same one. These ads can be personalized based on your visits to other websites. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can turn off personalized ads in Google's My Ad Center. Where the law requires it, you'll be asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

We don't share your Byome account, email, reviews or profile with advertisers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time from Edit profile. Your email, profile picture and profile details are removed. You choose whether your reviews, posts and comments are deleted too, or stay up as "Deleted member".

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
