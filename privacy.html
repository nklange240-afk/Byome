Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Byome ("we", "us") collects when you use byome.xyz, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we need to run Byome, and we don't sell your personal information.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, username, password (stored scrambled, so nobody, including us, can read it), and when you confirmed you're 18 or older and agreed to our terms.
  • Your profile, if you add them: a profile picture, ring color, bio, skin and hair details, your chosen biome, and your shelf.
  • What you post and do: reviews and review updates, posts, polls and votes, comments, "helpful" and likes, product suggestions, reports, points, achievements, shop purchases and shop visits (by the hour, for the Window Shopper achievement), starter checklist progress, and which achievement pop-ups you've seen.
  • Blocks: who you've blocked. This is private: the member you block isn't told.
  • Mod mail and feedback: messages you send the moderators, notes you add when blocking someone, and, for feedback, which Byome page you sent it from.
  • Feedback from visitors without an account: your message, the page you sent it from, and an email or other contact only if you choose to give one. To stop spam, we also keep a scrambled fingerprint of your internet address (never the address itself), which can't be turned back into the address.
  • Invites: your invite code, who invited you (if anyone) and who you've invited. So the same email can't be invited twice, we keep a scrambled one-way fingerprint of each email used to sign up. It can't be turned back into your email, and it stays even if you delete your account.
  • Error reports: if something breaks on a page, your browser quietly sends us the error message, the page, and your browser and device type (and your account, if you're logged in), so we can fix it. These are deleted after 30 days.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting and database providers automatically record basic technical details such as IP addresses and request times, for security, to prevent abuse, and to keep the site running.
  • On your device: your browser stores that you're logged in, your accessibility settings, and a few small preferences (like reminders you've hidden). This isn't sent to advertisers.

How we use it

  • To run Byome: your account, profile, posts, points, shelf and notifications.
  • To keep Byome safe: moderation, automod, reports, blocking, speed limits and preventing fake accounts.
  • To answer you: mod mail, feedback and emails you send us. If you send feedback without an account and leave a contact, we only use it to follow up on that feedback, never for marketing. Ask us at hello@byome.xyz to delete it any time.
  • To fix and improve the site: error reports and feedback.
  • To send account emails: confirming your email and resetting your password. We don't send marketing emails.

Byome is for adults

You need to be 18 or older to make an account, and we keep a record that you confirmed this when you signed up. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we'll delete the account and its information. If you believe a child is using Byome, please tell us at hello@byome.xyz.

Who can see it

  • Other members can see your username, profile picture, bio, profile details, reviews, posts, comments, shelf and achievements.
  • Private to you: your email address, points history, invites, blocks, reports you've made, and your mod mail. (Friends you invite can see that you invited them when they sign up.)
  • Moderators can see reports, content waiting for review, the moderation log, and all mod mail and feedback, so they can look after the community. They're asked to keep it confidential.
  • The site owner can see the database, including error reports, to run and fix Byome.
  • We may share information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or to investigate fraud or abuse. If Byome is ever transferred to a new owner, your information would move with it, under this policy.

Services we use

These companies help run Byome and handle information only to provide their service:

  • Supabase stores the site's data, profile pictures and handles logins.
  • Resend delivers mod mail marked urgent to the Byome inbox, so the team sees it quickly.
  • Resend sends our emails (account confirmation and password resets).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • Google AdSense shows the ads in the Byome Shop (see below).
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr provide fonts and a code library, so your browser connects to them when pages load.

These services may store information in the United States and other countries.

Ads in the Byome Shop

The Byome Shop shows ads from Google AdSense, inside the sign labeled "Advertisement." Ads appear only in the shop, never on Reviews, in the Community or anywhere else on Byome.

On the shop page only, Google may use cookies and similar technology to show ads, measure them, and limit how often you see the same one. These ads can be personalized based on your visits to other websites. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and you can turn off personalized ads in Google's My Ad Center. Where the law requires it, you'll be asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

We don't share your Byome account, email, reviews or profile with advertisers.

Byome counts how often the shop's signs are seen and tapped, as daily totals with no names attached. So that each member is only counted once a day, Byome notes that you've seen a sign that day; nobody can see these notes, and they're deleted after two days.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your account information while your account is open.
  • Error reports are deleted after 30 days.
  • When you delete your account, your email, login, profile picture, bio, profile details, points, achievements, shelf and notifications are removed. You choose whether your reviews, posts and comments are deleted too, or stay up as "Deleted member".
  • Some records may stay for a while afterwards: copies in backups, the email fingerprint used for invites, moderation records, and mod mail (shown as from "Deleted member"), to keep Byome safe and fair.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and change your profile any time on Edit profile, and delete your account there.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing hello@byome.xyz. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your information; we'll honor them as the law requires.
  • You can turn off personalized ads in Google's My Ad Center, and block or clear cookies in your browser.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable measures to protect your information: passwords are scrambled, connections are encrypted, and the database only lets each member read and change what they're allowed to. No website can be completely secure, though, so we can't guarantee it. Please use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and let members know on the site for important changes.

Contact

Questions about your information or this policy? Email us at hello@byome.xyz.
