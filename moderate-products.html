Moderation

Moderator guide: everything you can do

Thank you for helping look after Byome! Here's everything moderators can do, and how to do it properly. You earn 5 Byome points for each decision.

Tap a topic to open it.

The basics
  • Where to look: the number on Moderate in the menu is everything waiting for a moderator. Mod mail has its own number for messages waiting for a reply. You also get notifications.
  • Members never see which moderator acted. Messages come from "a moderator" or "the Byome moderators". Only moderators can see the moderation log at the bottom of this page.
  • Be fair and consistent. Judge what was written, not who wrote it. Negative reviews are welcome as long as they're honest.
  • Leave your own conflicts to another moderator: anything you wrote, anything about you, or someone you're in a disagreement with.
  • Keep it private. Don't share reports, mod mail or members' details outside the moderator team.
Held by automod
  • Posts, comments, reviews and review updates with a listed word or emoji are held automatically. Only the author and moderators can see them.
  • Approve false alarms (a beauty word that looks like a bad one, mild profanity that's not directed toward anyone, use your best judgement here). It goes live and the author is told.
  • Remove anything that breaks the rules, and choose a reason. The author is told why and keeps a private copy they can fix and resubmit.
Reports
  • Reports don't hide anything. Keep it if it's fine (that clears the reports), or Remove it with a reason.
  • Reported profiles: Remove picture, Remove bio or Reset username (they're asked to pick a new one). The member is told why.
Product suggestions: checking them properly
  1. Is it already on Byome? Watch for "Possible duplicate", and search Reviews for the brand and name. If it's already listed, use Merge (see "Merging repeat suggestions"); if someone suggested it just before, use Reject as duplicate.
  2. Is it spelled right? Check the brand's own website or a big retailer. The name and brand should match the packaging (capitals, accents, spacing). Fix small mistakes with Edit rather than rejecting.
  3. Category and subcategory: make sure they fit (a lip oil is Makeup › Lips, not Skincare). Change them with Edit if needed.
  4. Variations are real shades, scents or finishes only. Sizes like "Travel size" or "50ml" aren't variations: the page points them out, so edit them out. The variations the member entered earn them points when you approve (fixing their typo keeps it theirs), so remove any that aren't real. If they left variations out or wrote something like "28 shades", add the real names yourself: you earn points for each one you add (up to 10 per product).
  5. Photo link: it should be a picture of the product (from the brand or a retailer), not a link to the product page.
  6. Then Approve, or Reject with a reason. The member who submitted the suggestion will see your reason, so be clear and polite.
Merging repeat suggestions
  • Members can add a new product while writing a review. The suggestion then shows Comes with a review: approving it puts the review live too, and rejecting it sends the review back to the member, so their words aren't lost.
  • If the product is already on Byome, don't reject it: use Merge into [product] (or Merge into a listed product to search). Their review moves to the listed product, and any new shades can be added to it.
New shades and scents
  • Members can suggest a new shade or scent for a listed product. Check it's real (the brand's website or a big retailer) and spelled as on the packaging. Fix typos in the box before Approve; they keep the credit.
  • Sizes aren't variations. Reject with a reason (there are quick picks) if it isn't right.
Standout reviews
  • On Reviews, Mark standout appears on other members' reviews. Use it for reviews that are detailed, honest and genuinely helpful, positive or negative.
  • Standout reviews get a gold frame and tag, and the author earns bonus points. Use it thoughtfully, not for every good review. You can't mark your own.
  • You may use your own judgement in deciding what is a standout review, but try to keep it to no more than 1 in 10 reviews. This will be easier once we have more users posting reviews!
Community posts
  • Pin keeps an important post at the top of the Community (announcements, contests). Unpin it when it's done.
  • During an event, Feature puts a member's event post on the Events page and at the top of the Community.
Levels
  • Ready to Bloom: Saplings with 100 published reviews. Read a few of their reviews at random (the link opens their profile). There's no minimum length: look for reviews that are genuine and useful. Then Approve to Bloom, or Not yet with a note for the team (the member doesn't see it).
  • Level-up needs approval: members whose level was lowered by a moderator. Not yet notes here are shown to the member, so be kind and clear.
  • Change level (on a member's profile) moves someone down, with a reason they'll see. Use it rarely, for serious or repeated problems. You can't change your own level.
Restrictions
  • On a member's profile, Restrict pauses posting, commenting, writing reviews or suggesting products, for 7 days, 30 days or permanently, with a reason they'll see. Everything they've already shared stays up.
  • Use it for repeated or serious problems (spam, harassment, copied or fake reviews), not one-off mistakes: remove the content and explain instead.
  • They can't level up while restricted, and their next level-up waits for a moderator afterwards.
  • Any moderator can Lift it early, from their profile or the Restrictions list on this page. Moderators can't be restricted; tell the site owner if a moderator needs to step back.
Mod mail
  • Members can message the whole moderator team, send feedback, or add a note when they block someone. Every moderator sees every conversation.
  • Your replies show as from the Byome moderators, never your name. Be warm and brief, and say what happens next.
  • Mark done when it's handled. If the member replies, it opens again.
  • Bugs and typos from feedback: pass them on to me (the site owner), and I will fix them and give the finder credit. If a message goes unaddressed for more than a day, or if the bug poses a safety or security risk to anyone, please DM me (@reviewsbynoel or @joinbyome on Instagram) or email me (nklange240@gmail.com).
  • If someone may be in danger, or something illegal is involved, tell me (the site owner) straight away.
Feedback from visitors, and hidden messages
  • Visitors without an account can send feedback. It shows as From a visitor. They can't see replies on Byome, so if they left an email, reply from hello@byome.xyz; if not, just Mark done once it's read.
  • Mod mail with words automod would flag is hidden behind a note that says what kind of language it may contain. If you'd rather not see it, leave it for another moderator. That's always okay.
Not sure?

It's fine to leave something for another moderator, or ask the site owner. When in doubt, Keep rather than Remove, and explain kindly when you do remove something.
