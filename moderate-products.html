Moderation

Moderator guide: everything you can do

Thank you for helping look after Byome! Here's everything moderators can do, and how to do it well. You earn 5 Byome points for each decision.

The basics

  • Where to look: the number on Moderate in the menu is everything waiting for a moderator. Mod mail has its own number for messages waiting for a reply. You also get notifications.
  • Members never see which moderator acted. Messages come from "a moderator" or "the Byome moderators". Only moderators can see the moderation log at the bottom of this page.
  • Be fair and consistent. Judge what was written, not who wrote it. Negative reviews are welcome as long as they're honest.
  • Leave your own conflicts to another moderator: anything you wrote, anything about you, or someone you're in a disagreement with.
  • Keep it private. Don't share reports, mod mail or members' details outside the moderator team.

Held by automod

  • Posts, comments, reviews and review updates with a listed word or emoji are held automatically. Only the author and moderators can see them.
  • Approve false alarms (a beauty word that looks like a bad one, a quote, a joke between friends). It goes live and the author is told.
  • Remove anything that breaks the rules, and choose a reason. The author is told why and keeps a private copy they can fix and resubmit.

Reports

  • Reports don't hide anything. Keep it if it's fine (that clears the reports), or Remove it with a reason.
  • Reported profiles: Remove picture, Remove bio or Reset username (they're asked to pick a new one). The member is told why.
  • Lots of reports doesn't automatically mean it's bad, and one report can be serious. Read it yourself.

Product suggestions: checking them properly

  1. Is it already on Byome? Watch for "Possible duplicate", and search Reviews for the brand and name. If it's there, use Reject as duplicate.
  2. Is it real, and spelled right? Check the brand's own website or a big retailer. The name and brand should match the packaging (capitals, accents, spacing). Fix small mistakes with Edit rather than rejecting.
  3. Category and subcategory: make sure they fit (a lip oil is Makeup › Lips, not Skincare). Change them with Edit if needed.
  4. Variations are real shades, scents or finishes only. Sizes like "Travel size" or "50ml" aren't variations: the page points them out, so edit them out. Each approved variation earns the member points, so remove any that aren't real.
  5. Photo link: it should be a picture of the product (from the brand or a retailer), not something unrelated.
  6. Then Approve, or Reject with a reason. The quick picks fill in a friendly one, and the member sees it.

Standout reviews

  • On Reviews, Mark standout appears on other members' reviews. Use it for reviews that are detailed, honest and genuinely helpful, positive or negative.
  • Standout reviews get a gold frame and tag, and the author earns bonus points. Use it thoughtfully, not for every good review. You can't mark your own.

Community posts

  • Pin keeps an important post at the top of the Community (announcements, contests). Unpin it when it's done.
  • During an event, Feature puts a member's event post on the Events page and at the top of the Community.

Mod mail

  • Members can message the whole moderator team, send feedback, or add a note when they block someone. Every moderator sees every conversation.
  • Your replies show as from the Byome moderators, never your name. Be warm and brief, and say what happens next.
  • Mark done when it's handled. If the member replies, it opens again.
  • Bugs and typos from feedback: pass them on to the site owner, who can fix them and give the finder credit.
  • If someone may be in danger, or something illegal is involved, tell the site owner straight away.

Not sure?

It's fine to leave something for another moderator, or ask the site owner. When in doubt, Keep rather than Remove, and explain kindly when you do remove something.
