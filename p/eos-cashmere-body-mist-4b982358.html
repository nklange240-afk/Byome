A Holiday Treat

5 out of 5 leaves · Would repurchase

Right off the bat, this smells primarily of––what else?––peppermint, not chemicals, like some scents when first sprayed. It's a sweet peppermint, not a sharp peppermint. Like the other eos Cashmere Body Mists I've tried, it's inexpensive, does not irritate my sensitive arm skin (maybe just gives the slightest minty tingle!), and is good if you don't want a heavy scent that could overwhelm others.

Chai Scent for Fall

4 out of 5 leaves · Would repurchase

Sometimes, when I spray a fragrance, I smell chemicals first and foremost for a few moments. Not with this. I definitely get chai more than anything else, and I think I can pick up pumpkin, but it's much fainter. There's also a bit of a nuttiness to it, which may be the "warm nutmeg" base note. I spray it on my arms, and it does not bother my sensitive skin. It's very inexpensive and good if you want a scent that is lighter than perfume.

Great Summer Scent

When I spray this out, I don't notice a really chemical-y smell like I do with some fragrances at the very first moment. Instead, I get a whiff of raspberry, or maybe raspberry syrup, and something almost tropical, which may be the pomegranate, though they're not truly tropical, haha!
