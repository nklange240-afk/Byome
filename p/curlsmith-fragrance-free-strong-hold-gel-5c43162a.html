Amazing at first, then waves fall

3 out of 5 leaves · Wouldn't repurchase

At first, this gel gives me beautifully defined waves. And on paper, it should work really well for my fine, "swavy" hair that struggles to hold its wave pattern. Few moisturizing ingredients and very strong hold. But then, it seems that the very ingredients that cause the strong hold at first eventually prove to be too much weight for my hair, and my waves elongate. Which is really too bad, because this is fragrance-free, and I love fragrance-free hair products because then, if my hair touches my cheeks, my rosacea will be less likely to flare up worse.
