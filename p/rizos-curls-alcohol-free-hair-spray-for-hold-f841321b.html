Holding waves in place

4 out of 5 leaves · Would repurchase

This hairspray is a really good product if you're trying to get your curl or wave pattern to hold for a longer time. Though I prefer fragrance-free, it has a pleasant scent, and it is not sticky once dried. It's lightweight. One problem I do have with it is that if my hands are not bone-dry, as they generally are not when I'm in the middle of styling for wave definition, I struggle to use it one-handed, which makes it hard, because I need to use one hand to keep the spray out of my face as much as possible. My makeshift solution is to grip the bottle and nozzle using a dry washcloth.
