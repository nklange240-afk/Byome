Super Gentle Cleanser

5 out of 5 leaves · Holy grail · Would repurchase

I have dry, sensitive skin with rosacea, and I love BYOMA's Milky Moisture Cleanser! It's so gentle, but for me, still gets my face clean. It doesn't sting nor make my rosacea worse, and I can use it around my eyes. Note that it does not foam; it stays creamy, and it may not be the best option if you're oily. However, in my experience, if you are dry and just slightly acne-prone, it works without causing breakouts. When I wear makeup or water-resistant sunscreen, I use it as the second step in a double-cleanse.
