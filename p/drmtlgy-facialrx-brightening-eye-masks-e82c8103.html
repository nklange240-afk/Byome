4 out of 5 leaves · Wouldn't repurchase

These masks work wonderfully and have great results. They feel very hydrating and are gentle enough for sensitive skin. I’ve gifted them to several different people and been told by somebody that these are the only eye masks they’ve found that won’t irritate their skin. The only reason I won’t repurchase is that I don’t enjoy using them! Purely a personal preference, of course, but I’ve discovered that I don’t enjoy the feeling of wearing eye masks. I can feel them moving around any time I move my face and I feel like I can’t carry on with my day or do anything but lie still while using these.
