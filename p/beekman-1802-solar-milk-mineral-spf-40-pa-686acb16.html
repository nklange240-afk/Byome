Decent, but Beekman 1802 has better SPFs.

3 out of 5 leaves · Wouldn't repurchase

This sunscreen was decent, it had a relatively non-greasy texture and I could get away with the white cast since I have very fair skin, but I’ve tried better SPFs, including some of the other ones from Beekman 1802. While I did finish using the tube I had, I don’t plan to repurchase it. This could be good for somebody who required a mineral SPF, but otherwise the chemical SPFs they make have no cast and feel much more weightless on the skin.
