This is an interesting product! I think it’s worth trying once.

4 out of 5 leaves

This was gifted to me by the brand and the purpose of this product is to help your natural scent smell more neutral and balanced. It doesn’t burn or stink, safe for the underarms (test this if you have super sensitive skin just in case). It does dry down a bit sticky at first under the arms but it goes away. Now I’m a sweater and my natural musk shines through right after I shower, so things can go south quickly for me. This product honestly has helped neutralize the not so pleasant odors I produce. One night I decided to skip deodorant and just slept with this on, I woke up, let my mom smell my pits and she confirmed, there was no musty smell! I do like putting my deodorant on…
