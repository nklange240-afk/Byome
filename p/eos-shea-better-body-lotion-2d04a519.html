NEA-Accepted Body Lotion on a Budget

5 out of 5 leaves · Holy grail · Would repurchase

Okay, so admittedly, I can't tell a difference between this and the variation of the body lotion entitled "fragrance-free" (this one is also fragrance-free, in fact), but the differences marked by eos are that this one provides 48-hour instead of just 24-hour hydration, AND it bears the "Accepted" seal from the National Eczema Association. And I have to say, it IS great for eczema-prone skin. It's rich, it's thick, and unlike some similar-feeling lotions, you can apply a lot and it doesn't pill later. Plus, as I said in my review of the other variation, for a certified cruelty-free body lotion, the price is pretty unbeatable!
