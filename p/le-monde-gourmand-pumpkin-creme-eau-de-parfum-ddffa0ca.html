Not too sweet pumpkin

4 out of 5 leaves · Wouldn't repurchase

It may be a bit corny to say a pumpkin scent smells like pumpkin pie, but this kind of does! Besides pumpkin spice, it has notes of nutmeg and frothed milk with musk and sandalwood at the base. It's sweet but not overly sweet. The only reason I checked that I would not repurchase is because since I bought this last year, I've changed my preference to buying only certified cruelty-free beauty products, and Le Monde Gourmand, while they state that they're cruelty-free, is not certified. But if they did get certified, I would buy this again! While it's just one fl. oz., I paid less than $30 for it, so it's not super expensive, either.
