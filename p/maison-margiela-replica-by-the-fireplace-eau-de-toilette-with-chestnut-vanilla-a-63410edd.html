Great for cold weather!

4 out of 5 leaves · Would repurchase

This scent is great to wear during cold months. Very cozy and warm! However, I don't find it wearable during warm weather. The rich and smoky notes can easily become overpowering when warm. This is a scent i save for the cozy months, and i do look forward to using it! A little goes a long way- I usually need only one spray and get decent projection and longevity from it.
