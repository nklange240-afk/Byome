Affordable hair scent that's not for me

3 out of 5 leaves · Wouldn't repurchase

This is a hair mist that's meant to sprayed on the hair only, unlike Odele's other three fragrances, which are hair and body mists. It's super affordable, as I said in the title. I'm just not the biggest fan of this scent in my hair. The notes are oakmoss and ylang ylang, and while I I like ylang ylang essential oil, I think this combination is just too earthy, if that's the right word, for my tastes. But unlike me, if you find a lot of gourmand or fruity scents sickly-sweet, this might be right up your alley.
