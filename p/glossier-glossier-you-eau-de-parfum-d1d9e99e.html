My Signature Scent!

5 out of 5 leaves · Holy grail · Would repurchase

This has become my most-worn scent, mainly due to how subtle and delicate the scent feels. It opens with an almost effervescent pink pepper, before settling down into something slightly woody, a bit floral, and very musky. I’ve heard people say this scent reminds them of pencil shavings, and to be honest I 100% get that! However, for me that’s a positive association. This is the most complimented scent in my collection and I reach for it whenever I want a confidence boost or need to feel more like myself.

Noel · October 2, 2026
