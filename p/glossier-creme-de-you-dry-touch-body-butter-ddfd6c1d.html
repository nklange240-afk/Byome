Not as good as I had hoped.

3 out of 5 leaves

I adore the “Glossier You” fragrance, so I had high hopes for this. Unfortunately the cream has a bit of an odd scent at first. It does eventually settle into the “You” scent I’m familiar with, but the longevity is relatively short. The cream itself is non-greasy, which is a plus. I do love the packaging (heavy glass, feels very high quality), but wish it were refillable because it seems wasteful to buy another each time you run out.
