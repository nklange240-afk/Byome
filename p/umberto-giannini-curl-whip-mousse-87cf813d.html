Enhanced natural waves

4 out of 5 leaves · Would repurchase

I like this mousse. It is lightweight, with few heavy ingredients, which is what my super fine, barely wavy ("swavy") hair needs in a mousse which I'm going to use to enhance my wave pattern. What I do is spray in my heat protectant, glaze the mousse over my hair, scrunch, add a hairspray meant for wavy, curly, or coily hair, scrunch again, scrunch out excess water ("micro-plop") with a microfiber towel, and then, due to mobility limitations, rather than using my arms to diffuse, I put a mesh bonnet over my head to hold my waves close to my head and then over that, a diffuser cap that attaches to my dryer, and then diffuse. And with this mousse, when I'm done, my hair is undoubtedly wavier…
