Unique and niche

5 out of 5 leaves · Would repurchase

This smells exactly like a buttery pastry on me so I love it. Now this is not a safe blind buy, please test this on yourself because I know two people who thought they’d love it and ended up not liking it due to how it smelled on them. For me I get a sweet element from the jam note and it turns buttery from there. This is an EDT so the lasting power is shorter lived than a regular EDP. Also I feel the only scents that’ll go with it are other SNIF products like Berry styles, maybe extra whip so don’t go layering this with just anything. I’m not one to follow the “rule” of what’s a summer fragrance and what’s a fall fragrance but this one I make an exception for.
